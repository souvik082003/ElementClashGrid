How to Play 📖

  • Pick a cell: Click any empty cell to target it.
  • Play a move: Select Rock, Paper, or Scissors before time runs out. The AI secretly does the same!
  • Battle!: The winner of the RPS clash claims the cell.
  • Win the Grid: Claim 3 cells in a row (horizontal, vertical, diagonal).
  • Power Cells ⚡: Win these on rounds 3, 6, 9 to earn an ultimate Wild Card!
