# Element Clash Grid

**Rock Paper Scissors × Tic Tac Toe — Strategy Edition**

A highly interactive and strategic web game built entirely with Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Pick a cell:** Click any empty cell on the 3x3 grid to start an RPS battle for it.
2. **Choose your move:** Pick ✊ Rock, ✋ Paper, or ✌️ Scissors before the 5 second Battle Timer runs out!
3. **Resolution:** The AI chooses its move simultaneously. 
   - Winner claims the cell!
   - Ties leave the cell empty and up for grabs again.
4. **Power Cells (⚡):** Every 3rd round (3, 6, 9), a random empty cell becomes a Power Cell. If you win it, you receive a **Wild Card**, which is an ultimate move that absolutely guarantees a win over any standard Rock/Paper/Scissors move.
5. **Win the grid:** Get 3 of your cells in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
6. **Win the match:** Win 2 grids to dominate the entire match! Best of 3 Grids.

## 🤖 AI Difficulty Levels
- **Easy:** Plays completely randomly. Great for relaxing or learning.
- **Medium:** Uses basic Tic-Tac-Toe block/win strategy but plays its Rock/Paper/Scissors moves randomly.
- **Hard:** Tracks your previous move patterns. If you lean too heavily on "Rock", the Hard AI will mathematically predict "Paper"! It also aggressively counters your 3-in-a-row attempts.

## ✨ Features
- Fully responsive Vanilla design
- Glowing visual particles on win
